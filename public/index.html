About

Full-stack roots. Platform brain. AI lab energy.

My path went from building client-facing web apps to leading DevOps, platform, observability, security, and infrastructure teams. That mix still shows up in how I work: I care about systems being reliable, but I also care about the interface humans use to understand them.

Lately I have been pushing hard into AI-assisted operations: local LLM routing, compiled knowledge wikis, OpenClaw/QMD memory, source ledgers, and automation loops that can help run a home or engineering org without losing the plot.

Being a father of two also changed what I want software to do. A lot of my homelab apps exist because I want family life to run smoother: coaching notes, learning games, speech practice, printable maker ideas, growth tracking, and small workflows that turn daily chaos into something useful.
